## Method

- One completion per case is generated once, then scored by both judges.
- Exact agreement is the fraction of cases with identical 1-5 scores.
- Within-1 agreement allows a one-point difference.
- Cohen's kappa is unweighted over the five rubric levels; it corrects for agreement expected by chance.
- Kappa is unstable on small samples; treat fewer than 30 scored cases as directional only.
